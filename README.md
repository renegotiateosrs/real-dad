# Real Dad

Your dad now watches you play Old School RuneScape. He has notes.

## What he does

- **Approval rating (0–100).** Saved per account, so he holds a grudge between sessions. Shown in an overlay with a mood: *Brags at the pub*, *Almost proud*, *Tolerating you*, *Sighing loudly*, *Googling adoption law*.
- **Level ups.** He reacts to every 10th level and sometimes to others, usually by comparing you to your cousin (default name "Kevin", you can change it).
- **99s.** He gets emotional and gains a lot of approval.
- **Deaths.** −8 approval and something hurtful.
- **Pets.** Sets approval to 100. He always believed in you. Delete the chat logs.
- **Collection log slots and valuable drops.** He asks whether it pays rent.
- **Bank.** At most every 15 minutes, he roasts you if you have under 1M gp, or asks why you still owe him money if you have over 100M.
- **AFK.** If you don't touch the mouse or keyboard for a while (4 minutes by default), he notices.
- **Session length.** Hourly updates on how long you've been playing and how long the lawn has gone unmowed.
- **Bedtime.** Between midnight and 5am local time he tells you to go to bed.
- **Public chat.** He sometimes asks who you're talking to and whether they have a job.
- **Overhead replies.** Your character mumbles "sorry dad" over their head. Only you can see it.

## He goes out for milk

If approval reaches **0**, Dad goes out for milk. The overlay counts how long he's been gone. While he's away he is silent, apart from the odd postcard.

He comes back when you:
- get a 99,
- get a pet, or
- pass a new multiple of 100 total level.

They were out of 2%.

## Config

| Option | Default |
|---|---|
| Cousin's name | Kevin |
| Show approval overlay | on |
| Overhead replies | on |
| AFK nag after | 4 minutes (0 = off) |
| Bedtime enforcement | on |
| Chatter chance | 35% |
| Dad's chat colour | brown |

## Plugin Hub compliance

Everything happens on the client and is cosmetic. There are no menu swaps, no input automation and no network calls. The overhead text is set only on your local player model, so nobody else sees it.
